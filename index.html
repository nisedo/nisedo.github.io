About

I’m nisedo, a security researcher at Trail of Bits. I audit smart contracts and the infrastructure that runs them, across blockchain ecosystems.

I’ve worked in technology consulting since 2017 and specialized in blockchain security since 2023. EVM contracts were my starting point but my work has since expanded to other platforms, nodes, and broader systems.

These days, I’m especially drawn to fuzzing, formal verification and agentic security research.

I also founded Soliditors, a French-speaking Web3 security community where researchers connect and share knowledge.
