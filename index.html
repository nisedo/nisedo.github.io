About

I’m nisedo, a security engineer at Trail of Bits. I audit smart contracts and the infrastructure that runs them, across blockchain ecosystems.

I’ve worked in technology consulting since 2017 and specialized in blockchain security since 2023. EVM contracts were my starting point but my work has since expanded to other platforms, nodes, and broader systems.

My current research focuses on developing and evaluating AI-assisted methods for vulnerability discovery, patch generation, and secure software development.

I founded and lead Soliditors, a French-speaking Web3 security community of 200+ members. I’ve hosted 10 podcast episodes and co-organized events with iExec and Ethereum France.
